# Prior art — and the structural gap

An honest map of what exists, what it does well, and why none of it is The Witness.
The gap is not intelligence, empathy simulation, or data collection. The gap is that
**every system below keeps the person's self in memory that someone can edit.**

## LLM companion apps
Persistent-memory chatbots that "remember" the user and develop a relationship.
- **Strength:** real comfort for lonely people; low friction.
- **Failure for this use:** the memory is mutable cloud state owned by the vendor.
  Model updates change personality (users have publicly grieved a companion's
  overnight personality change after a vendor update). A dementia patient's
  "self" cannot live somewhere a product manager can patch.

## Memoir / life-story apps
Guided prompts, recorded stories, printed books for family.
- **Strength:** dignified, human, proven appetite.
- **Failure:** a record, not an agent. It cannot judge a new claim, refuse an
  action, intervene in a scam at 2 a.m., or label the provenance of a story a
  relative later adds.

## Digital legacy features (platform settings)
Designated contacts who can access accounts after death.
- **Strength:** solves real access pain.
- **Failure:** purely post-death and purely about accounts. Protects nothing
  during the years of decline, holds no validated self, carries no directives
  it can act on.

## Advance directives, POA, guardianship law
The legal layer — and The Witness is designed to *serve* it, not replace it.
- **Strength:** legal force; centuries of precedent.
- **Failure:** paper is static and silent. It cannot notice a coached wire
  transfer, cannot testify at the moment of the act, and is routinely contested
  or simply not found in time. The Witness's role: hold the person's *own*
  directives in a form that can actually speak at the moment they're violated,
  and produce an audit trail a court can use.

## Bank / card fraud detection
Statistical anomaly scoring on transactions.
- **Strength:** catches real fraud at scale.
- **Failure:** knows transactions, not values. It cannot distinguish "unusual
  but *hers*" (a spontaneous gift she always wanted to give) from "unusual and
  coached." Its false positives punish autonomy; its false negatives are the
  $28B headline. A values-based gate — *her* rules, written by her — is a
  different instrument, and the two should work together.

## AI "griefbots" / digital afterlife services
Chatbots trained on a deceased person's messages to simulate conversation.
- **Strength:** some families report comfort.
- **Failure:** trained to *resemble*, optimized for engagement, with no duty to
  the person's actual recorded beliefs. They will improvise new "memories,"
  new opinions, new forgiveness, new blame — in the dead person's voice. The
  Witness's anti-fabrication clause exists because this failure is not a bug
  in those products; it is their business model.

## The gap, stated plainly

No existing system combines:
1. a self **grown with the person** over years (not scraped after diagnosis),
2. an **immutable core** with a two-person change ceremony that *ends* at the
   competence boundary,
3. **provenance on every memory**, with other people's stories structurally
   barred from becoming the person's past,
4. an execution layer that can **refuse** in the person's own name, and
5. a post-death steward bound to **refuse fabrication**.

That combination is what the ATMAN architecture was already designed for. The
Witness is what it is *for*.
